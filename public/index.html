How to play

Black moves first. Players alternate placing stones on empty intersections. The first unbroken line of five or more—horizontal, vertical, or diagonal—wins.

Keyboard: focus the board, use arrow keys to move, then press Enter or Space.

Select any empty intersection to place a stone
